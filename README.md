# Pharmacy Sales & Profitability Dashboard

## Power BI | #dataDNA Challenge

#### 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Pharmacy Sales & Profitability Dashboard developed in Power BI as part of the #dataDNA challenge.

The dashboard analyzes €8.63M in total revenue and €2.42M in total margin, delivering an overall margin rate of 28.04%. It is designed to transform raw transactional sales data into clear, actionable business insights through structured storytelling and intuitive visualization.

### 🎯 Objectives

The primary goal of this dashboard is to:

Provide an executive-level overview of financial performance

Identify revenue and margin drivers across markets

Evaluate promotion effectiveness

Compare performance by brand, category, and pharmacy type

Enable dynamic filtering for deeper analytical exploration

### 📈 Key Insights

Strong overall margin performance (~28%) across markets

Germany, France, and Italy lead in revenue contribution

Non-promoted products contribute significantly to total margin

Urban pharmacies generate the highest sales volume

Clear variation between high-volume and high-profit brands
